Untapped energy,
developed in full.

It starts with finding the right match — a site, and the technology built for it.
From there we take it through development and construction, and stay with it once it's running.

01 — Originate

We identify the opportunity.
On both sides.

One side: a site wasting energy inside infrastructure it already runs. The other: equipment already proven and running at commercial scale.

We're technology-agnostic, with direct relationships across every major provider — so the site determines the equipment, not the other way around.

02 — Develop

Full project execution. No capital required from the asset owner.

Once we've identified a viable match, we take the project through everything required to reach operation: technology selection, technical assessment, permitting, offtake contracts, and financing.

This is the stage where most potential projects stall: permitting takes time, financing requires relationships, and contracts must be negotiated across multiple parties. We own that process, and carry it through financial close and into construction.

03 — Operate

We hold the asset for the life of the project.

We take full responsibility for its performance, maintenance, and revenue. The host infrastructure keeps running exactly as before; the equipment operates alongside it as an independently managed asset.
