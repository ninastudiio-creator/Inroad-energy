The Resource

The energy is already there.

There is enormous energy being generated and wasted every day inside existing infrastructure — in gas pipelines, water networks, industrial facilities, and organic waste streams.

Our Approach

We capture it.

The most efficient source of clean energy isn't new capacity built from scratch — it's the energy already flowing through existing infrastructure.
