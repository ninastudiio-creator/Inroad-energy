Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026

Inroad Energy Ltd, a private company registered in Israel under company number 517360244 ("Inroad", "we", "us"), operates this website and is the controller of any personal information described here. This policy explains what happens to information when you visit it. It is short because this site does very little with data.

1. We do not collect personal information through this site

This website has no contact forms, no sign-up fields, no newsletter subscription, and no user accounts. There is nothing here for you to submit, and we do not ask you for your name, email address, or any other personal detail in order to browse it.

2. We do not use cookies or tracking

This site sets no cookies. It uses no analytics service, no advertising pixels, and no third-party tracking or profiling tools of any kind. We do not build a profile of you, and we do not know who you are when you visit.

3. No third-party requests

At the time of publication, all core website assets — including the typeface, images, and video — are served from our own domain, and the site does not intentionally load third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking resources.

4. Server logs

Like virtually all websites, the servers that host this site automatically record standard technical information when a page is requested — typically the IP address, browser type, referring page, and time of the request. These logs are maintained by our hosting provider as part of normal website operation, for security, diagnostics, and reliability, and are retained only for those purposes. We do not use them to identify individual visitors.

5. If you contact us

The contact details on this site are ordinary email addresses. If you choose to write to us, we will hold your message and contact details for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and to keep a record of our business correspondence. We do not add correspondents to any marketing list.

6. Links to other sites

This site links to external destinations, including our LinkedIn page. Once you follow such a link you are on a service operated by someone else, governed by their privacy policy rather than ours.

7. Your rights

Where applicable under local law, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted. Because this website collects nothing, such a request would relate only to correspondence you have sent us directly. To make a request, write to us at the address below.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how this site works — for example by adding a contact form or an analytics tool — we will update this page and revise the date at the top.

9. Contact

Questions regarding this policy or the way we handle personal information can be directed to Omer Agadi at omer@inroad.energy or on +972 54-569-6372.
